🗺 6-Month Learning Phases

1

Month 1–2 · Build the Foundation

Focus: Vocabulary, basic grammar, listening
  • Learn 10 new words daily using Anki flashcards
  • Watch English YouTube with subtitles (BBC, TED-Ed)
  • Read newsinlevels.com at your comfort level daily
  • Practice shadowing — repeat after native speakers
  • Study basic grammar: tenses, articles, prepositions
2

Month 3–4 · Start Speaking

Focus: Conversation, pronunciation, writing
  • Join Hilokal or Tandem — talk to native speakers daily
  • Join English Discord servers and chat actively
  • Write short paragraphs about your daily work
  • Record yourself speaking, then review and improve
  • Learn IT/Tech vocabulary (meetings, PRs, standups)
3

Month 5 · Professional English

Focus: Work emails, interviews, LinkedIn
  • Learn how to write professional emails and Slack messages
  • Practice answering common interview questions in English
  • Rewrite your CV and LinkedIn profile in strong English
  • Watch mock interview videos and imitate the style
  • Study phrases like "I'd like to follow up…", "As per our call…"
4

Month 6 · Apply & Practice Under Pressure

Focus: Real interviews, real applications
  • Apply to remote jobs — treat rejections as practice
  • Do mock interviews on Pramp or with friends
  • Attend English-speaking meetups (online or local)
  • Write LinkedIn posts about your tech journey in English
  • Aim for 1 real interview per week to build confidence
